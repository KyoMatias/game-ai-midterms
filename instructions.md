**Official Instructions for Midterms**

Recreate the mechanics shown in the video and add these rules:

There must be at least one additional colored opponent (minimum of 2 total opponents). These opponents should also collect wandering AI units. Once collected, the wandering AI will follow their respective leader AI. Each leader AI must actively search for and collect other wandering AI units.

When two leader AIs collide (including the player), the leader with fewer followers will automatically be destroyed.

Opponent AIs should attempt to chase the player if they have significantly more followers. If the player has more followers than them, they should avoid or flee from the player.
